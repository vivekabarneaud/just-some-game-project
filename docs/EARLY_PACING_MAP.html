Where the three breadcrumbs slot

Two are clean fixes to the defense lane. The third is the one you flagged to sequence last — it needs a design call first.

Defense · fixes Gap 1

1 · Re-gate the Watchtower

Move Eyes on the Horizon to fire on walls built (the_first_threat done) instead of raid-survived. The watchtower now follows the walls naturally, no raid required.

Baptism of Fire stays as a parallel, optional beat — it completes whenever a raid eventually does resolve, and keeps its reward. No content lost, lane unblocked.

Defense · fills empty Ch.2 + teaches training

2 · Man the Wall

A new Chapter-2 defense quest after the watchtower: train the watch. Teaches the startTraining garrison mechanic that nothing currently introduces, and gives the empty chapter real content.

"Stone keeps nothing out on its own. It wants men on it who know the horizon."

Population · sequence last · needs a call

3 · Room to Grow

With one citizen, births can't even fire (needs ≥2 adults + food surplus + happiness). Growth comes from arrivals + housing, not a single button.

So: a soft overview nudge ("we are too few"), or a real quest? And what action closes it — build houses to a level, or run the recruitment mission? This is the one to decide before writing.

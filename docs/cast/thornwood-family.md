# The Thornwoods — Family Canon
- **Status:** locked 2026-06-28
- **Members:** Nessa (char_000, archer, ~33), Gareth (char_005, archer, ~31), Godric (char_021, warrior, ~21), and **Jack** (adopted, ~7, settlement NPC).
- **Origin:** Ashwick / the Hearthlands. Always-poor tenant farmers.
- **Role in the game:** They are the **"hunters family"** whose arrival unlocks the Adventurer's Guild. See the Overview flavor `hunters_arriving` (ch.2) and `hunters_settling` (built when hunting camp + houses exist): *"the new families brought us hunters"*, *"a new child runs the camp, smaller than Nell and louder"*, *"the ration count is heavier by four"* — **3 adults + 1 child = four**. They arrive already reunited, as a family.

## The spine: a family that gives what it doesn't have
The whole family is one idea: **both parents died giving, and the children became people who give.** Dirt-poor their entire lives, they understand hunger from the inside, so they take people in, they protect, and **they will not harm the helpless.** Both brothers deserted the army rather than kill defenseless people — that is the family signature, not a coincidence. The adopted boy is this ethos made flesh.

## The parents (both "died giving")
- **The mother.** Poor and generous. She spent her strength and her last coins on **honey and sweet things** so her children would taste sweetness in a life that had none, working herself ragged to afford it (mending and washing at night, eating less so they ate more). She died **bringing Godric into the world** — a body already spent by years of giving. *(Mechanical echo: Nessa and Gareth both have the `sweet` food preference — her sweet things, a sensory ghost. Godric is `spicy`: he never knew her, never tasted her honey.)*
- **The father.** Quiet, worn, fundamentally decent; loved in deeds, not words. His one human flaw: he could **never quite look at Godric**, the baby whose birth cost him his wife — so **Nessa became Godric's real mother.** He died in **the killing winter**, having given his food to the children: he ate last and least and the cold took a man with nothing left in him.

## The childhood
Always-poor tenant **barley farm** in the Hearthlands. Everyone worked, children included. The mother died when **Nessa was ~12** (Gareth ~10, Godric a newborn), and from that morning Nessa was the mother of the house — raised Godric, decided who ate in the lean years.

## The catastrophe — "that winter" (~5 years ago)
Tithe and rent alone don't break a family; a **disaster** does, and the system that won't forgive a dead season finishes it. An early, killing winter: stores ran out, they ate the seed-grain to survive, which meant no spring crop, which meant no rent — and **the rent did not soften for a dead season, so they lost the land.** The **father died** that winter. This is the hinge of everything.

## Jack (~7 now — settlement NPC)
Orphaned by **the same winter** (a neighbour's child, parents dead of cold and hunger), taken in by the grieving Thornwoods in that same black season: **they buried one soul and took in another.** Too young to be marked by it — he is **loud, bright, alive** (canon: *"smaller than Nell and louder"*), and knows no other family. **Nessa is his mother in all but blood.**
- **Deliberately NOT** from any burned village or massacre — decoupled from Gareth's war on purpose, so he stays the loud, untraumatized camp-kid the Overview flavor describes (a massacre survivor would be withdrawn like Nell, not loud).
- **He is the family's answer to death** — proof they refuse to only shrink. That is his meaning, and it's enough; he needs no dramatic origin.

## The scattering and the lost years
- **Gareth** enlisted as a professional soldier **before** the winter (eldest son sending coin home), so he was **away on the northern marches** when his father and the farm were lost — a distance he has never forgiven himself.
- **Godric** was **levied** (conscripted) at **16, right after** the winter — a grieving boy grabbed for the Nordveld war.
- **Nessa** was the fixed point: she took the orphaned boy and **drifted**, landless, surviving by the bow (poaching, hunting, seasonal field-work), always moving — a woman alone with a child draws questions, and poaching is a hanging crime. She kept him fed and hidden through ~5 homeless years.
- **The desertions (~3 years ago).** Godric broke fast (~2 years of front-line killing, deserted at ~18). Gareth held until the one order he could not obey (the marksman task, below), then deserted. **Desertion is a capital crime** — they became hunted men who could not go home.
- **The reunion.** When the winter scattered them the understanding was *go south, to the frontier, where land might be had and the Crown's reach is thin.* Home stopped being a place (the farm is gone) and became **Nessa**. The two deserter brothers made for the same horizon and found their way back to her on the frontier roads. They arrive at the settlement **already reunited, a family of four.** That arrival **is** "the hunters family."

## Gareth's village (the deed he refused)
A military **reprisal** — the army's work, *not* the Inquisition's: a poor border village said to **shelter Nordveld raiders** was to be made an example of. As the best bow in the company, Gareth was handed the marksman's task — **ring the burning village and shoot anyone who fled.** He refused to turn his aim on fleeing families who looked like his own, let them scatter into the dark (saving no one he could name), and walked. *(These were likely Old-Faith border folk — Netheron-revering, Nordveld-adjacent kin, the same stock as Edda's grandmother — which is why they were easy to brand as collaborators. The army's stated reason is military; the religious dimension is the unspoken backdrop.)*

## Latent hooks (for later)
- **The brothers are wanted deserters.** If the Dominion's reach ever extends to the frontier (tax collectors, Radiant Knights, the Inquisition — which the story implies comes), Gareth and Godric are in danger. A future tension lever.
- **The boy as Nell's foil** (loud vs. silent). Long arc: a child may reach Nell where the adults (only Jory + Edda) could not. Cross-ref Nell.
- **Edda's Nordveld blood** ([[founder_edda]]): she would understand exactly what was done to the kind of border folk Gareth's reprisal destroyed. Optional resonance.
- **Village name for Gareth's reprisal: TBD.** Candidates: **Barrowfen**, **Greycairn**, **Coldbeck** (the first two carry a quiet Old-Faith/burial resonance). Kept out of his bio (vague "a border village") until named.

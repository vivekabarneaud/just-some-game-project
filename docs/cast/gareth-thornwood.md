## Recruit-card bio (public surface)
> Gareth went for a soldier before the bad winter came, the eldest son sending coin home to a farm that could not feed three, and he was good at it, the marching and the bow both, the best shot in his company. He was away on the northern marches when the winter took his father and the land, and he has never forgiven himself the distance. Then his company was sent to make an example of a border village said to shelter Nordveld raiders, and because he was the best bow they handed him the worst task: to ring the place and put down anyone who ran from the fire. He would not turn his aim on fleeing families who looked exactly like his own. He let them scatter into the dark, saved not one soul he could name, and walked, which makes him a deserter the Crown would hang. He keeps the company's tin badge in his boot, not from loyalty but because throwing it out would mean deciding what that day meant. He laughs easily and starts fights over anyone being pushed around; his sister says those are the same thing in him.

## Deep lore (discovery-only)
- **The wound is the limits of conscience.** His refusal was clean — he wouldn't shoot fleeing families — but it **saved no one he could name.** The village burned anyway; the only thing his conscience bought was chaos enough to let some scatter. He kept his hands clean in a fire he could not put out. That bitterness, not heroism, is what he carries.
- **The distance.** He was away soldiering when the winter killed his father and took the farm. He has never forgiven himself for not being there.
- Distinct from Godric: Gareth refused **at the threshold** (a single deed he would not do); Godric crossed the line many times and broke from the weight. Two brothers, same family ethos (will not harm the helpless), different breaking points.

## Combat identity & talents (parked 2026-06-29)
**Archetype: Elite Marksman / Sharpshooter (archer) — the disciplined soldier-bow.** Big **single-target** damage, **focus-fire** (rarely switches targets), **high crit, low miss, armor-pierce**, and uniquely a **heavier archer** (can wear chainmail — the trained soldier survives the front, unlike Brenna's light woodland kit). His "starts fights over anyone pushed around" bleeds into a **peel / cover-the-weak** angle. Talent model = Godric's template (see [godric-thornwood.md](godric-thornwood.md)).
**Tree layout (2026-06-29):**
- *Signature passive (free, L1):* **Focused Aim** — each consecutive turn he attacks the *same* target, +damage (stacks; resets on switch). His "rarely switches targets" discipline.
- **Tier 1 — Foundations** (minors): **Steady Hand** (+crit/rank), **Bowman's Strength** (+single-target dmg/rank).
- **Tier 2 — Soldier's Craft**: **Soldier's Discipline** (ranked — +mitigation; the tanky armored archer), **Suppressing Fire** ⭐ (peel — pull/suppress an enemy targeting a weaker ally).
- **Tier 3 — Marksmanship**: **Aimed Shot** ⭐ (a charged, big single-target hit) + the **pierce-vs-crit** mini-fork: **Piercing Shot** (ranked — ignore armor) vs **Deadeye** (ranked — +crit).
- **Tier 4 — the fork**: **Sharpshooter** ⭐ (glass-cannon — max burst, amps Aimed Shot + crit/pierce; but his damage draws aggro, so he *depends on the team to peel for him*) **OR** **Linebreaker** ⭐ (durable — tankiness + upgraded Suppressing Fire; self-sufficient, eats the aggro his damage earns and shields others). *The fork's real meaning: glass-cannon-needs-protection vs durable-and-independent.*
- **Tier 5 — capstones**: **Best Bow in the Company** ⭐ (the marksman ultimate — Focused Aim / Aimed Shot reaches devastating output) · **Nobody Touches Them** ⭐ (loyalty-gated — when an ally is downed or cornered, Gareth's protective fury spikes his damage *and* auto-peels whoever's hitting the weak one). *(Reframed from an earlier "Won't Abandon Them": a damage-vs-"won't-kill-the-fleeing" choice was a non-choice — the restriction just read as a downside. His conscience is now a purely beneficial protective burst; "won't fire on fleeing families" stays as flavor, not a mechanical tax.)*

**Chainmail / tankiness (decided 2026-06-29):** NOT gated — he can wear armor from the start (his ex-soldier identity; Brenna stays light). The point of his tankiness *talents* (Soldier's Discipline, Linebreaker) is that **his own high single-target damage generates aggro** (threat system), so without durability he gets hunted and needs Godric to peel; with it he's self-sufficient. Tankiness is the investable counterweight to his own threat — that's the soul of the Sharpshooter/Linebreaker fork.
- *Minors:* +crit, +armor-pierce, +single-target dmg, +mitigation.

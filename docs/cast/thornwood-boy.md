# The Thornwood Boy (name TBD)
- **Status:** in progress (concept locked, name pending)
- **Recruit ID:** none — **settlement NPC** (like Nell), not a recruitable adventurer (too young to fight)
- **Age:** ~7 (canon: *"smaller than Nell and louder"*; Nell is 11)
- **Family:** Adopted by the Thornwoods. Brenna is his mother in all but blood. See [thornwood-family.md](thornwood-family.md).

## Who he is
A child the **killing winter orphaned** (~5 years ago) — a neighbour's boy whose parents died of cold and hunger. The grieving Thornwoods, burying their own father that same season, took him in: **they buried one soul and took in another.** He was ~2 then, too young to be marked by any of it. He has been a Thornwood for ~5 years and **knows no other family.**

## Why he matters (his meaning)
He is **the family's answer to death** — the proof that a household stripped of everything refused to only shrink. He embodies the parents' "died giving" ethos, carried forward by the children the moment they were orphaned themselves. He needs no dramatic origin; *that* is his weight.

## Personality
**Loud, bright, alive** — he "runs the camp," louder than anyone, the opposite of silent Nell. Resilient, untroubled, the family's spark of noise. (This is *why* he is deliberately **not** a massacre survivor — a traumatized child would be withdrawn like Nell, which would contradict the canon flavor that establishes him as loud.)

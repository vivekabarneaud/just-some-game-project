# DESIGN: Crafting & Armor Progression (leather → mail → plate)

**Status:** Principle, adopted 2026-07-26. Complements [[DESIGN_NOVICE_ITEMS]] (which covers the day-one loadout + rarity=stat-budget) and the enemy audit method's loot loop.

**One-line:** Armor advances by **material + craft complexity**, gated by **building level + resources** — never by hard slot-locks. You start ragged and *earn* your kit; the "you don't get good head/leg armor for a while" feel emerges from the world, not an arbitrary rule.

---

## The three armour tiers

| Tier | Material | Where | When | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hide / leather** | wolfhide, bristlehide, plain leather | **Leatherworking** | early–mid | the frontier hunter's armour — skin what you kill |
| **Mail** | iron rings | **Blacksmith** + iron | mid | the blacksmith's *starting* armour line |
| **Plate** | steel plates | **Blacksmith (high level)** + **steel** + **town tier** | late | the wealth marker — a knight's harness cost a farm |

Historically accurate (mail predates plate by centuries; plate was late-medieval and elite) and it makes progression *earned*. **The early blacksmith makes mail, NOT plate.** Plate is a genuine milestone. Lore hook: plate can carry a **Khazdurim** (master-smith) flavour, arriving with that relationship — it's not just "smith level 5," it's advanced craft.

## Gate by the world, not by slot-locks

Do **not** hard-lock slots (WoW's old "no helm until level X"). It reads as arbitrary ("my smith can forge a sword but not a cap?"). Instead let **material + building + resource** requirements produce the pacing:

- **Leather cap** — craftable once you have Leatherworking (early–mid).
- **Mail coif** — needs the Blacksmith + iron (mid).
- **Plate helm** — needs the master smith + steel + town (late).

The player still fills the kit *gradually* — crude hide → mail → plate — but because the *good* versions are genuinely later-tier crafts, not because a slot is forbidden. More immersive, same pacing.

## Interaction with the day-one loadout (from DESIGN_NOVICE_ITEMS)

- **Day one:** a few **weapons + chest + cloak**. Head / legs / boots arrive **guild L2–3, not day-one** (soft pacing via recipe requirements — you need the shop + hunted mats).
- **Rarity = stat budget:** **common = 0 stat bonus** (function only — a weapon's damage, an armour's DEF); stats (a +1 primary, a raw sub-stat) require **uncommon+**.

## Applied

- The wolf/boar **hide** pieces are the *leather* tier — early–mid, Leatherworking, uncommon (+1 stat + a raw sub-stat). Consistent.
- Their **mail** and **plate** equivalents (if ever) are separate, later lines at the Blacksmith — not made from hide mats.
- A hide **cap** (e.g. the boar's tusked head-piece) is fine at guild L2–3. A **plate helm** is not — that's the late tier.
